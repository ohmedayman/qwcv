Frequently Asked Questions

What is ATS and why do I need an ATS-compatible CV?▼

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is an automated system used by large companies to filter and sort resumes. QCV ensures your resume is compatible with these systems to get you to the interview.

Is the free plan enough to build a professional CV?▼

Yes! The free plan gives you everything you need to build one professional resume with a basic template and PDF download.

How does the AI assistant work in QCV?▼

QCV's smart assistant analyzes your resume content and suggests instant improvements like better phrasing for your professional summary.

Can I cancel my QCV subscription anytime?▼

Yes, you can cancel your subscription anytime without any extra fees. Your data will remain saved.

Is my data safe on QCV?▼
