The Hiring Bottleneck

Why Resumes Get Rejected & How QCV Solves It

Most job seekers invest massive effort applying, only to be silently rejected by automated software.

The Problem: Why Scanners Fail You

  • Complex Graphical Elements: Heavy tables, multi-column shapes, and icon blocks confuse parser bots.
  • Missing Key Requisites: Lack of standardized keywords and measurable metrics leaves your CV ranked at the bottom.
  • Flat Image PDFs: Converting pages into rasterized canvas images breaks text extraction completely.

The Solution: How QCV Drives Interviews

  • Clean Linear Architecture: Standard single/double column structures easily digested by ATS and humans alike.
  • Intelligent AI Enhancer: Auto-generates quantifiable impact statements and suggests missing keywords for every job ad.
  • True Vector Text PDF: Embedded searchable typography with smart pagination that prevents split paragraphs.
