Cover Letter Structure

Every effective cover letter follows a proven structure. Here is how your letter is built.

Hook

A strong opening that states the position you want and immediately captures attention.

Body

The heart of the letter — highlighting your top skill and one concrete achievement that proves your value.

Call to Action

A confident closing that invites the hiring manager to get in touch with you.

Tips for a Strong Cover Letter

Simple guidelines to help your letter stand out from the rest.

  • Start with a strong hook — state the role you want and why you are the right fit right away.
  • Lead with your top skill and back it up with one concrete, measurable achievement.
  • Keep it short — one page is enough to make a strong impression.
  • Use action words and numbers to show real results.
  • Read your letter once more, then copy it straight into your application.
