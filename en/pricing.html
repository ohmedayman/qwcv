Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about QCV plans and billing.

What's included in the Free plan?
The Free plan lets you build 1 professional CV with a classic template and export it as a PDF (with watermark). It's free forever and requires no card. Upgrade to Pro to unlock 30+ ATS templates, PDF without watermark, and the full AI toolkit.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts, so you can cancel anytime from your account. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.
Do you offer refunds?
If you're not satisfied, contact our support team on WhatsApp right after purchasing and we'll help you with a refund on a case-by-case basis. We aim to make every purchase risk-free.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Vodafone Cash, Fawry, and Instapay. After checkout, you'll receive a confirmation on WhatsApp and your plan is activated within a few hours.
Do you have team or company plans?
Yes — the Unlimited plan is designed for companies and large teams. For custom pricing or invoicing for larger organizations, contact us on WhatsApp and we'll set you up.
