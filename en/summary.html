What makes a great summary

Four simple rules to make your opening statement stand out to recruiters and ATS systems.

Use keywords

Mirror the language of the job posting. Recruiters and ATS software scan for the exact skills and terms mentioned in the role.

Add numbers

Quantify your impact. Percentages, revenue figures, and timeframes turn vague statements into measurable proof of results.

Start with action verbs

Open with words like "led", "built", "grew", or "managed". Strong verbs instantly communicate confidence and momentum.

Keep it to 3-4 sentences

Recruiters skim CVs in seconds. A short, punchy summary gets read; save the details for your experience section.
