Frequently Asked Questions

The questions our users ask the most

How do I create a new QCV account?
You can sign up with your email or using your Google account. Click "Start Free" on the homepage, choose the method you prefer and enter your details. The whole process takes less than a minute.
Is QCV free?
Yes! Our free plan lets you build one resume with basic ATS templates and PDF download. You can upgrade to the Professional plan to unlock more features.
How do I start building my resume?
After signing in, you will reach the editor. Start by entering your personal information (name, job title, contact details), then add your professional summary, experience, education and skills. Every change appears instantly in the live preview.
Is there a mobile app for QCV?
QCV is currently available as a web app that works in your browser. You can use it on mobile, tablet and desktop. A native mobile app is planned for the future.
Is my data safe with QCV?
Yes! All your data is securely stored on Firebase Cloud with advanced encryption. We never share your data with any third party. You can delete your account at any time.
How do I change my password?
From the login page, click "Forgot password", enter your email and you will receive a password reset message. If you have a Google account, your password is managed by Google.
How do I delete my account?
From the profile dropdown menu, choose "Account Settings", then click "Delete Account". Warning: this action cannot be undone and your data will be permanently deleted.
How do I change the app language?
From the profile dropdown menu, choose "Language". We support English, Arabic, French, German and Turkish.
Can I use QCV on more than one device?
Yes! Your data is saved in the cloud, so you can use QCV from any device by signing in with your account. All changes are saved automatically.
How do I upgrade my plan?
From the profile dropdown menu, choose "Billing & Subscriptions", then select the plan you want and follow the payment steps.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept payment via Vodafone Cash, Fawry and InstaPay. After payment, your request is reviewed by our team and your plan is activated as soon as it is approved.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. No extra charges will apply. Your data will remain saved and you can keep using the free plan.
What is the difference between the Professional and Unlimited plans?
The Professional plan includes 5 resumes and advanced templates. The Unlimited plan includes unlimited resumes + 24/7 support + advanced ATS analysis.
The page is not working for me, what should I do?
Try these steps: 1) Update your browser to the latest version, 2) clear your browser cache, 3) try another browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge), 4) make sure you are connected to the internet. If the problem persists, contact us.
I can't export my resume to PDF
Make sure your resume has enough content, and try reducing the font size or margins using the controls at the top. If the problem persists, try using the Chrome browser.
My changes are not being saved
Make sure you are signed in to your account. If you are using offline mode, changes are saved locally and synced once you reconnect to the internet. Also make sure your browser allows cookies.

Step-by-Step Guides

Follow these simple guides to get the most out of QCV

Build Your First Resume

1. Sign in to your account and open the Resume Builder.

2. Enter your personal information: name, job title and contact details.

3. Add your professional summary, work experience, education and skills.

4. Watch every change appear live in the preview, then save your resume.

Analyze, Export & Share

1. Open any resume and click the ATS Analysis button to check your score.

2. Review the suggestions and apply the recommended keywords and fixes.

3. Click Export and download your resume as a high-quality PDF.

4. Share your resume with a link or send it straight to recruiters.
