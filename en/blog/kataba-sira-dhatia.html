How to Write a Resume from Scratch — Step by Step

January 10, 2025 · 12 min read

Writing a professional resume can feel like a daunting task, especially the first time. But with the right steps and good examples, you can create a resume that grabs hiring managers' attention and earns you the interview. In this guide, we walk through building your resume from scratch, step by step.

Why Your Resume Matters

Your resume is your first impression — make it count.

Your resume is the first impression you leave with an employer. In just 6-7 seconds, a manager scans your resume and decides whether you are a fit. It must be clear, focused, and professional.

Key Fact

An average of 250 resumes is received for every job posting. Yours must stand out within the first seconds.

Step 1: Contact Information

Make it clear, accurate, and easy to find at the top of your resume.

Required Details

  • Full name: Write it exactly as it appears on official documents.
  • Job title: The position you are applying for or your current role.
  • Email: Use a professional address (Gmail is preferred).
  • Phone number: Make sure it is correct and include the country code.
  • City and country: Where you currently work or your target city.
  • LinkedIn URL: Your profile link (linkedin.com/in/username).

Example

Ahmed Mohamed Ali
Front-End Developer
ahmed.ali@gmail.com | +20 100 123 4567
Cairo, Egypt
linkedin.com/in/ahmedali

Contact Tips

  • Do not write your full home address.
  • Avoid unprofessional emails such as "cuteboy99@gmail.com".
  • Include the country code in your phone number.
  • Add your GitHub profile link if you are a developer.

Step 2: Professional Summary

The first thing managers read after your name.

How to Write an Effective Summary

  • Start with your job title and years of experience.
  • Mention your biggest achievement or distinguishing skill.
  • Use numbers and statistics where possible.
  • Keep it concise and professional in 3-4 lines.

Example Summary

Front-end developer with 5 years of experience building interactive websites and web apps. Specialized in React and Vue.js. Improved page load speed by 40% and increased conversion rates by 25% for two companies.

Golden Tip

Tailor the summary to every job you apply for. Review the job ad and use its keywords in your summary.

Step 3: Work Experience

The most important section of your resume.

For Each Job, Include

  • Job title: The official title of the position.
  • Company name: The full company name.
  • Duration: From date to date (month/year).
  • Achievements: 3-5 accomplishments with numbers and metrics.

Experience Example

Senior Front-End Developer
Modern Technology Company | January 2021 - Present

  • Built UI for a mobile app used by over 100,000 active users.
  • Reduced page load time from 5 seconds to under 1 second.
  • Mentored a team of 4 junior developers on best practices.

Writing Achievements with Numbers

  • ❌ Weak: "Helped improve site performance."
  • ✅ Strong: "Improved site performance, increasing load speed by 50%."
  • ❌ Weak: "Managed a team."
  • ✅ Strong: "Managed a team of 8 and delivered the project in 3 months instead of 5."

Step 4: Education & Qualifications

Show your academic background and complete your professional picture.

Required Information

  • Degree: Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD.
  • Major: Your primary field of study.
  • University: The name of the institution.
  • Graduation year: The year you earned the degree.
  • GPA: Only if it is excellent (3.5+ or 85%+).

Education Example

Bachelor of Computer Engineering
Cairo University | 2019
GPA: 3.8/4.0

When to Put Education First

  • If you are a fresh graduate with little professional experience.
  • If the degree is required for the role (medicine, engineering, accounting).
  • If you earned an excellent GPA or graduated from a prestigious university.

Step 5: Skills & Competencies

Show recruiters you have what it takes to succeed.

Technical Skills

  • Programming: JavaScript, Python, Java, C++
  • CMS: WordPress, Drupal
  • Design tools: Figma, Adobe XD, Photoshop
  • Databases: MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB

Personal Skills

  • Leadership and team management
  • Effective communication
  • Problem solving
  • Time management
  • Working under pressure

Important Tip

Avoid listing overly generic skills like "Microsoft Office" unless the job ad explicitly asks for them. Focus on skills relevant to the position.

Tips to Improve Your Resume

Small refinements make a big difference.

Formatting Tips

  • Use standard fonts like Arial or Calibri at 10-12pt.
  • Leave enough white space for easy reading.
  • Use headings and categories to organize information.
  • Keep it to one or two pages maximum.

Content Tips

  • Use past tense for achievements ("built", "managed", "improved").
  • Focus on achievements, not daily tasks.
  • Avoid spelling and grammar mistakes.
  • Tailor your resume to each job.
  • Never write "References available on request".

Final Check Before Sending

A short checklist to keep you out of trouble.

  • Read your resume out loud to check the flow of the language.
  • Ask someone else to review it.
  • Check for spelling and grammar errors.
  • Verify all dates are correct.
  • Confirm your contact information is accurate.
  • Save the file as a PDF.
