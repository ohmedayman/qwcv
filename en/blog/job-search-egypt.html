How to Find a Job in Egypt in 2025 — Best Sites and Strategies

January 15, 2025 · 14 min read

Egypt's job market is evolving fast, with growing sectors and new opportunities appearing every day. Whether you are a fresh graduate or an experienced professional, this guide gives you a complete roadmap for finding a job in Egypt in 2025 — the best sites, proven strategies, and tips to stand out from the competition.

Top 20 Job Platforms in Egypt

Start your search on the right platform to save time and reach the best opportunities.

Main Search Platforms

  • Wuzzuf.net: Egypt's largest job platform with over 100,000 active jobs, an easy interface, and advanced search filters.
  • Forasna.com: A popular Egyptian platform offering a wide range of jobs across all governorates.
  • LinkedIn: The world's largest professional network. Excellent for networking and finding fresh opportunities.
  • Indeed.com/eg: A global job search engine with a dedicated Egypt section.
  • Bayt.com: A leading Arabic job platform that also covers Egypt.

Specialist Platforms

  • Careers.eeeg.com: Focused on engineering and technical jobs.
  • EgyptJobs.net: A local platform dedicated to jobs within Egypt.
  • Gradua.com: Specializes in jobs for new graduates.
  • Hult.com/jobs: International opportunities open to Egyptians.
  • Glassdoor: Browse company reviews and salary insights.

Major Company Career Portals

  • Vodafone Egypt Careers: Opportunities at Vodafone Egypt.
  • Orange Egypt Jobs: Open roles at Orange Egypt.
  • CIB Careers: Roles at the Commercial International Bank.
  • Samsung MENA Careers: Samsung positions across the Middle East.
  • Siemens Egypt: Opportunities at Siemens Egypt.

Social Media & More

  • Facebook job groups: Communities like "Jobs in Egypt" and "Work Opportunities Egypt".
  • Twitter/X: Follow recruiting accounts such as @Wuzzuf and @Forasna.
  • Telegram channels: Job channels like "Egypt Jobs".
  • Other useful sites: Jobzella.com, Shaghaf.com, Mostaql.com, and Khadamty.com for services and freelance work.

An Effective Application Strategy

Applying is not about quantity — it is about being prepared at every stage.

Before You Apply

  • Search carefully: Do not apply to every job. Focus on roles that match your skills and experience.
  • Research the company: Learn about its activity, culture, and values before applying.
  • Tailor your resume: Adjust your resume to fit each job's requirements.
  • Write a custom cover letter: Explain why you are the right person for this specific role.

While Applying

  • Follow instructions precisely: Some companies require specific file formats or extra information.
  • Apply early: Submit at the start of the posting window to improve your chances.
  • Verify your details: Your phone number and email must be correct and active.
  • Attach high-quality files: Make sure your PDFs are clear and not corrupted.

After Applying

  • Follow up: Send a follow-up email one week after applying.
  • Prepare for interviews: Research common interview questions and practice your answers.
  • Stay persistent: Applying to 10-20 jobs per week is a healthy pace.

Effective Networking

Professional networking is a secret weapon in any job search.

Networking on LinkedIn

  • Complete your profile: A professional photo, clear headline, and strong summary.
  • Join professional groups: Communities related to your field.
  • Share valuable content: Articles, insights, and professional experience.
  • Engage with others: Comment and share others' content constructively.
  • Connect with professionals: Send personalized connection requests with a note.

Direct Networking

  • Attend job fairs, conferences, and career events.
  • Join business clubs and professional communities.
  • Volunteer on community projects to open professional doors.
  • Stay in touch with former colleagues who can recommend you.

Recruitment Agencies & Job Fairs

Use every channel available to maximize your chances.

Top Recruitment Agencies

  • Adecco Egypt: One of the largest global recruitment firms in Egypt.
  • Manpower Egypt: A worldwide agency with deep local experience.
  • Hays Egypt: Specializes in professional and executive recruiting.
  • Michael Page Egypt: Focused on management and specialist roles.
  • Robert Half Egypt: Specializes in finance and accounting.

Key Job Fairs

  • Egypt Job Fair: Annual event in Cairo bringing together hundreds of companies.
  • Cairo ICT Job Fair: Dedicated to information technology roles.
  • Graduate Employment Fair: Focused on graduate jobs.
  • University career days: Events hosted by major universities.

Tips for Making the Most of Job Fairs

  • Dress professionally in a suit or formal attire.
  • Carry plenty of resume copies (20-30).
  • Research participating companies in advance and prepare for them.
  • Ask smart questions about the company and open roles.
  • Collect business cards and follow up after the fair.

Most Hiring Sectors in Egypt in 2025

These industries are creating the most opportunities right now.

IT & Technology

Software and app development, data analysis and AI, cybersecurity, and web and mobile development.

Marketing & Sales

Digital marketing and social media, content writing and management, PR and advertising, and market research.

Finance & Accounting

Accounting and financial reporting, financial analysis and planning, banking services, and risk management.

Engineering & Manufacturing

Mechatronics and automation, construction engineering, renewable energy, and engineering project management.

Healthcare

Nursing and medicine, hospital management, pharmaceuticals and medical sales, and medical technology.

Preparing Your Resume for the Egyptian Market

Local employers value resumes that speak their language.

  • Write in Arabic and English: Most companies in Egypt prefer a bilingual resume.
  • Mention your location: State your governorate and your willingness to relocate if needed.
  • List your nationality: Important information for most Egyptian employers.
  • Highlight languages: English, French, and German are in demand at many companies.
  • Mention military service: A required detail for men in Egypt.
